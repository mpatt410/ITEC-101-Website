The Banking Industry

The banking industry plays huge role in the economy by giving people an outlet to store money, whilst accuring interest revenue. Banks also are able to provide loans which provide people the opprotunity to invest in business ideas or plans that promote economic growth. Banks can also provide support when it comes to advising financial decisions especially in times of critical need or crisis.

Cybersecurity in Banking

Two very common cybersecurity attacks in banking include:

  • Phishing Attacks: Scammers who try to steal or breach your private information like account credentials throught tactics such as fake emails and websites.
  • Ransomware: Malicious software can lock a bank's systems and demand payment to restore access.

Cybersecurity Saftey Practices:

  • Use strong passwords containing many characters and special symbols and two-factor authentication.
  • Make sure all software and systems updated on a regular basis.
  • Teach staff and clients about online threats and how to handle or even avoid them entirely.

Job of a Financial Advisor

Financial advisor's forms personal connections with clients and helps them with anything regarding their personal finances and decisions. Some ways financial advisors assit people with their money is through teaching them proper saving and spending habits, investment strategies, and helping them plan for future expenses like retirement.

Ethical Issues That Arise in the Banking Industry

One commong ethical issue that often occurs in banks is when a financial advisor recommends something that is more beneficial to the bank than the client. This issue can cause distrust with the bank and the people. Examples of this issue are:

  • Advisors recommending high-fee mutual funds or insurance policies.
  • Encouraging unnecessary loans or credit products that could boost the banks profitability rather than the clients.

Despite this common ethical issue clients can protect themselves by becoming financially literate and reviewing terms and options with multiple people.
